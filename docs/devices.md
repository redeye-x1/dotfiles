- The +1 Challenge: habit app with progressive daily challenges. Expo + SQLite, local-first (`~/development/projects/the_plus_1_challenge`).
- Ricochet: 2D geometric puzzle game; place reflectors to bounce a ball to the target. Unity (`~/development/projects/ricochet`).
